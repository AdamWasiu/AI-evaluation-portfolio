# AI Image Evaluation and Visual Consistency

## Project Overview

**Project type:** Independent portfolio demonstration  
**Focus area:** AI Evaluation and Multimodal Quality Assessment

This case study demonstrates a structured approach to evaluating AI-generated images for instruction adherence, visual consistency, text accuracy, and potential generation artifacts.

## Objective

The objective is to assess whether an AI-generated image accurately follows a given prompt and whether the resulting visual content is coherent, readable, and free from noticeable inconsistencies.

## Evaluation Methodology

### 1. Instruction Adherence

- Compare the generated image against the original prompt.
- Identify required objects, attributes, relationships, and scene details.
- Record missing elements or details that contradict the instructions.

### 2. Visual Consistency

- Inspect object shapes, proportions, anatomy, and spatial relationships.
- Look for duplicated, distorted, or implausible visual elements.
- Distinguish genuine inconsistencies from acceptable artistic stylization.

### 3. Text and OCR Verification

- Inspect text appearing within the image.
- Compare visible text with any wording required by the prompt.
- Flag misspellings, malformed characters, illegible text, or omitted wording.

### 4. Quality and Artifact Assessment

- Examine image clarity, rendering defects, unnatural edges, and visual distortions.
- Determine whether any defects materially affect the image's meaning or usability.
- Avoid treating minor stylistic differences as critical failures.

## Demonstration Scenario

**Example prompt:** A red ceramic mug sits on a wooden desk beside an open notebook, with morning sunlight entering through a nearby window.

The following observations illustrate how an evaluator could assess a hypothetical generated image.

| Evaluation criterion | Example observation |
|---|---|
| Prompt adherence | Check that the mug, desk, notebook, and window are present. |
| Attribute accuracy | Verify that the mug is red and the notebook is open. |
| Spatial consistency | Inspect whether the objects occupy plausible positions. |
| Lighting | Check whether the shadows and highlights are consistent with the depicted sunlight. |
| Visual artifacts | Inspect the mug handle, notebook edges, and object boundaries for distortions. |

*Note: This is an illustrative scenario, not a claim that an actual image was tested.*

## Findings and Reporting

A useful evaluation report should identify:

- The specific instruction or visual requirement being assessed.
- The observed issue, if any.
- The evidence supporting the observation.
- The likely impact on image quality or prompt compliance.
- The recommended disposition, such as pass, flag for review, or fail against the applicable criteria.

## Quality Principles

My evaluation approach emphasizes evidence-based judgments, consistent application of guidelines, careful verification, and clear documentation of uncertainty.

Findings should be reproducible wherever possible, and ambiguous cases should be escalated rather than resolved through unsupported assumptions.

## Limitations

The demonstration uses a hypothetical prompt and does not claim measured performance, benchmark results, or outcomes from a production evaluation dataset.
